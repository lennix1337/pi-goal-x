- Drafting tool error contract (AXI follow-up, `extensions/goal-drafting.ts`): red at the real executed boundary first (hermetic harness, throwing `fetch` trap, fixture store): every failure return of `goal_question`, `goal_questionnaire` and `propose_goal_draft` lacked `isError` (6 of 9 new contract tests failed; the save-failure, non-error and successful-create guards were already green). Cancellation, decline, Continue refining and focus loss (before the dialog or during confirmation) stay plain results; the post-confirmation guard was split so only focus loss remains non-error. A failing goal save throws and rejects natively (draft preserved, no goal file) and was left unwrapped to avoid claiming non-creation when a throw could follow the write. Findings: `Completed goals cannot be tweaked` is only reachable with a pinned in-memory record because disk reconciliation unfocuses completed goals; new goals still get 30 runs and no token cap, configured defaults stay inherited (existing tests green). Not changed: headless `goal_question` still reports `cancelled` without a dialog host (existing contract).
